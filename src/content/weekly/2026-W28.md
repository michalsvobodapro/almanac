    with insulin resistance as an independent predictor), and now gut-microbiota-mediated
    osteoporosis — all show up in the periodontium. The practical shift: document
    periodontal findings in every medically complex patient and communicate them to
    their physician. That is no longer soft advocacy — it is RCT-backed care.'

    — domain-trained or retrieval-augmented systems earn their place; general-purpose
    LLMs do not. The LLM-powered Socratic endodontic tutor (RCT-validated) is the
    constructive exception: AI as a reasoning scaffold for students, not a diagnostic
    oracle.'
