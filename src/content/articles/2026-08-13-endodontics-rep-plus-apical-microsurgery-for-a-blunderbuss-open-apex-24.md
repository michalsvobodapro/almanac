summaryDeep: 'The blunderbuss open apex — a wide, divergent apical foramen — is one
  of the harder endodontic scenarios because conventional obturation is unreliable
  and apexification with MTA is technique-sensitive. This case combines a regenerative
  protocol (blood clot scaffold, bioceramic barrier) with apical microsurgery as a
  rescue layer, and follows the tooth for 24 months using CBCT, periapical radiography,
  and laser Doppler flowmetry. At 24 months, the tooth is functional, periapical healing
  is evident, and there are signs of continued root development. The multimodal assessment
  protocol is the most transferable element — it''s a model for how to document complex
  endo cases rigorously. The clinical limitation is obvious: n=1. As the IEJ translation-gap
  review in today''s digest argues, single cases are not a substitute for the translational
  groundwork regenerative endo still needs. File this as a technique reference, not
  a protocol.'
