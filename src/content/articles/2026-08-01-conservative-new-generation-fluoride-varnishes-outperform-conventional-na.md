summaryDeep: 'White spot lesions in primary teeth represent an early, reversible stage
  of caries that is frequently undertreated. This systematic review searched MEDLINE
  and the Cochrane Library, applying ROB2 and GRADE assessments to 7 qualifying studies.
  Varnishes with bioactive additives — including CPP-ACP, silver compounds, and novel
  fluoride complexes — showed statistically significant reductions in ICDAS scores,
  while conventional 5% sodium fluoride varnishes showed more modest effects. Intensive
  application protocols (more frequent reapplication) consistently outperformed standard
  schedules. The review is limited by the small number of included studies and heterogeneity
  in varnish compositions, application protocols, and follow-up durations. For pediatric
  dental practice, the takeaway is actionable: when managing active white spot lesions
  in primary teeth, choose a bioactive or next-generation fluoride varnish over standard
  NaF, and apply it more frequently than the typical twice-yearly schedule.'
