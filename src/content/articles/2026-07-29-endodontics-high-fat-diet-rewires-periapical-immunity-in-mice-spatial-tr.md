summaryDeep: 'Spatial transcriptomics allows gene expression to be mapped to specific
  tissue locations, giving this study an unusually granular view of how metabolic
  stress reshapes the periapical immune microenvironment. The high-fat diet model
  produced shifts in macrophage polarisation and inflammatory mediator profiles consistent
  with exaggerated bone resorption — a plausible mechanism for the clinically observed
  association between obesity/metabolic syndrome and worse endodontic outcomes. As
  a mouse lab study, the evidence grade is low: species differences in immune architecture,
  the artificial nature of the HFD model, and the absence of any clinical correlation
  limit direct application. What it does is provide mechanistic scaffolding for future
  human studies and raises the question of whether metabolic optimisation before endodontic
  treatment could improve healing — a hypothesis worth watching.'
