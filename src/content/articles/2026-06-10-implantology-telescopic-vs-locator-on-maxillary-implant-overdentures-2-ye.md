summaryDeep: 'The underlying RCT by Ibrahim et al. in the Journal of Prosthetic Dentistry
  is one of the few randomized trials to directly pit telescopic crowns against LOCATOR
  attachments specifically in the maxilla — a site where retention is notoriously
  more challenging due to unfavorable lever mechanics and reduced bone volume. Over
  24 months, the trial tracked both objective retention force and radiographic marginal
  bone loss, giving a dual endpoint that reflects both patient-relevant function and
  biological cost. This EBD commentary synthesizes the findings: telescopic attachments
  appear to offer superior long-term retention but come with greater technical complexity
  and cost, while LOCATORs are simpler to maintain but may show more bone loss at
  the implant neck under certain loading conditions. For a prosthodontics rotation,
  the takeaway is that attachment selection for maxillary overdentures should be individualized
  — bone quantity, patient dexterity, and maintenance access all factor in. A clean,
  clinically actionable read.'
