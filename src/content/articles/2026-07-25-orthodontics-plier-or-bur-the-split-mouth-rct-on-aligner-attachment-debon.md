summaryDeep: 'As clear aligner therapy becomes the dominant orthodontic modality,
  the debonding appointment is increasingly a routine clinical event — yet evidence
  on technique has been sparse. This split-mouth RCT randomised 32 patients'' quadrants
  to either adhesive removal pliers or a 24-blade carbide bur, with plier cases requiring
  a secondary bur pass for adhesive cleanup. Removal time per attachment was not significantly
  different between groups, which is clinically reassuring. The pain difference in
  lower posterior segments (NRS median 2.0 bur vs. 1.0 plier, p=0.028) is modest but
  real — posterior mandibular attachments sit close to the gingival margin and bur
  vibration is more perceptible. The adhesive remnant finding — more score-1 remnants
  (adhesive on tooth, none on bracket) with optimised versus conventional attachments
  after plier debonding — suggests attachment geometry influences how cleanly the
  bond fractures. For students about to start aligner cases: pliers first, bur for
  cleanup, and warn patients about posterior sensitivity.'
