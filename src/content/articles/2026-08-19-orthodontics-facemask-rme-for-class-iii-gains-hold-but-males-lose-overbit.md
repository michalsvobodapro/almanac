summaryDeep: 'The study tracked lateral cephalometric changes at pre-treatment (T0),
  post-treatment (T1), and follow-up (T2, mean 17.9 months) in 33 growing patients
  (mean age 10.9 years) with skeletal Class III and maxillary deficiency. ANB and
  Wits improved significantly and held at T2, confirming that early FM+RME intervention
  produces durable sagittal correction in this window. The mandibular plane angle
  increase during treatment is a known trade-off and did not fully resolve at follow-up
  — relevant for high-angle patients. The sex-specific overbite finding is novel and
  clinically actionable: male patients may need additional vertical control strategies
  or closer monitoring post-retention. The retrospective design and modest n=33 limit
  generalizability, but the sex-disaggregated analysis adds granularity that most
  FM studies lack. For orthodontic students, this reinforces the importance of early
  intervention timing and sex-aware treatment planning in Class III cases.'
