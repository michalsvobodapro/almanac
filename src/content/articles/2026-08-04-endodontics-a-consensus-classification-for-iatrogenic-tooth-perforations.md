summaryDeep: 'Iatrogenic perforations are one of the most consequential procedural
  complications in endodontics, yet the field has lacked a unified classification
  that captures location, size, timing, and contamination status in a clinically actionable
  way. This consensus-derived system addresses all those dimensions through a structured
  multi-phase methodology, giving clinicians a shared vocabulary for case documentation
  and referral, and giving researchers a standardised outcome variable. The practical
  upside is immediate: a classification that maps to prognosis allows more honest
  consent conversations and cleaner indications for repair versus extraction. For
  students, this is the kind of foundational taxonomy paper worth bookmarking — it
  will almost certainly appear in future ESE position statements and postgraduate
  curricula. It also pairs naturally with the new CBCT follow-up guidelines published
