summaryDeep: 'Class II furcation management has long been a judgment call dressed
  up as a clinical decision. This paper attempts to systematize that judgment by extracting
  variables with documented influence on regenerative outcomes and maintenance success
  from PubMed, EBSCO, and Scopus, then grouping them into two scored axes. The Morphological
  Regenerability Score (−5 to +9) captures vertical furcation component, root divergence
  at crestal bone, entrance width, defect location, root trunk length, and anatomical
  modifiers like enamel pearls and cervical projections. The Maintenance Accessibility
  Score (0–1) integrates patient compliance, plaque control, smoking, diabetes, tissue
  phenotype, and endodontic status. The framework is explicitly conceptual — thresholds
  are derived from reported effect sizes, not from a prospective validation cohort.
  That caveat matters: treat it as a structured checklist that forces you to consider
  all relevant variables, not as a scoring system with proven predictive accuracy.
  For a student learning to think about furcation prognosis, the two-axis structure
  is pedagogically valuable.'
