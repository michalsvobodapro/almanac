summary: In 60 consecutive orthognathic patients, fully customized lingual appliances
  (CCLAs) achieved significantly lower ABO CRE scores than prefabricated vestibular
  multibracket appliances at both the pre-surgical and final assessments, with the
  largest differences in alignment and intermaxillary criteria. The efficiency advantage

summaryDeep: Pre-surgical orthodontic decompensation is where orthognathic outcomes
  are won or lost, and this retrospective cohort of 60 patients (30 CCLA, 30 PVMA)
  uses the digital ABO CRE scoring system — an objective, validated metric — to compare
  the two approaches. CCLAs scored roughly 9 points lower (better) than PVMAs at both
  intermediate and final assessments, with alignment criteria driving the gap. Treatment
  efficiency during decompensation also favored CCLAs. The study is retrospective
  and single-center, and the CCLA group had an additional setup model included in
  planning, which may confound the comparison. Cost and laboratory turnaround for
  fully customized lingual systems remain significant barriers in many European settings,
  including Czech Republic. Still, for complex orthognathic cases where decompensation
  precision is paramount, the evidence is trending toward customized systems. This
  is the kind of data that should inform referral decisions when planning combined
  surgical-orthodontic treatment.
