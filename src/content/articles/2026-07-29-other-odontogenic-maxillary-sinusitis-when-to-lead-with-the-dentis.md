summaryDeep: 'Odontogenic maxillary sinusitis sits at the intersection of dentistry

  review stratifies management by dental treatability, sinonasal symptom burden, CT/endoscopic
  extent, disease duration, and presence of oroantral communication or fistula. The
  numbers are striking: in symptomatic patients who started with dental treatment,
  81% still required ESS — suggesting dental-first is often just a delay. Primary
