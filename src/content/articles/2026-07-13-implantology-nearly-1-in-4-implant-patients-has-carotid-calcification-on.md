summaryDeep: 'Panoramic radiographs are taken routinely before implant placement,
  yet the soft-tissue calcifications visible in the carotid region are frequently
  ignored. This retrospective study of 254 Turkish implant patients (κ=0.86 intra-observer
  agreement) found CAC in nearly one in four — a prevalence consistent with other
  Mediterranean cohorts. The logistic regression odds ratios are striking: hypertension
  OR 48, diabetes OR 44, cardiovascular disease OR 41, hyperlipidaemia OR 33. These
  figures reflect the strong confounding between systemic disease burden and calcification
  risk rather than independent causal effects, but the clinical implication is clear.
  Dentists reviewing OPGs before implant surgery are in a position to flag incidental
  CAC and prompt medical referral — a genuine opportunity for interprofessional care.
  The cross-sectional design cannot establish causality, and the single-centre Turkish
  sample limits generalisability, but the prevalence finding alone justifies a systematic
  look at the carotid region on every pre-implant OPG.'
