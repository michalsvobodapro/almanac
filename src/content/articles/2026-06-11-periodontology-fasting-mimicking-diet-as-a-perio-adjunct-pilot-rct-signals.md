summary: A multi-centre feasibility RCT tested whether cycles of a fasting-mimicking
  diet (FMD) alongside non-surgical periodontal treatment could shift the inflammatory
  response — specifically CRP — beyond what scaling alone achieves. It's a pilot,

  meaningful for patients who are both periodontally and metabolically compromised.
summaryDeep: 'Non-surgical periodontal therapy reliably reduces local inflammation,
  but systemic inflammatory markers often remain stubbornly elevated — particularly
  in patients with metabolic comorbidities. This multi-centre feasibility RCT is one
  of the first to test whether a structured fasting-mimicking diet, used as a dietary
  adjunct during the active treatment phase, can amplify the systemic anti-inflammatory
  response, with CRP as the primary inflammatory readout. Being a pilot, the trial
  was powered for feasibility rather than definitive efficacy, so the numbers should
  be read as hypothesis-generating rather than practice-changing. That said, the FMD
  protocol is well-characterised in the metabolic literature, and its translation
  into a periodontal adjunct context is a genuinely novel angle. For students, this
  is a preview of where perio research is heading: away from purely local interventions
  and toward systemic lifestyle co-interventions. Watch for the full-powered RCT that
  will inevitably follow.'
