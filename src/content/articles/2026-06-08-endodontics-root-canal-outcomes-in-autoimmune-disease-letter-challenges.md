summaryDeep: 'The original study examined whether autoimmune disease status affects
  the success rate of root canal treatment — a clinically relevant question given
  that immunomodulatory drugs and systemic inflammation both plausibly affect periapical
  healing. This correspondence challenges aspects of that retrospective design, likely
  around case definition, confounding by medication type, or outcome measurement.
  For a student, the pairing of this letter with the SSc case report above is instructive:
  the field is actively trying to characterise how systemic immune conditions interact
  with endodontic biology, and the evidence base is still thin and contested. The
  practical upshot is caution: do not assume standard healing timelines in patients
  on biologics or with active autoimmune disease, and document baseline status carefully
  for follow-up comparison.'
