summaryDeep: 'The BDJ news item is brief, but the underlying German study adds to
  a now substantial body of evidence connecting periodontal inflammation to systemic
  organ pathology. The kidney link is biologically plausible: chronic bacteremia from
  periodontal pockets, systemic inflammatory cytokine load, and shared risk factors
  (diabetes, hypertension) all provide mechanistic scaffolding. What distinguishes
  this study is the use of bleeding on probing — a direct clinical sign — rather than
  radiographic bone loss or self-reported gum disease, which strengthens the exposure
  measurement. For the periodontology-oriented student, this reinforces the argument
  that treating periodontal disease is not cosmetic or purely local: it is a systemic
  health intervention. The full study details are not available in the excerpt, so
  the effect size and study design cannot be fully evaluated here.'
