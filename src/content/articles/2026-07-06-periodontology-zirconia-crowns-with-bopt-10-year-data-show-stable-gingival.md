summaryDeep: 'BOPT (Biologically Oriented Preparation Technique) prepares teeth with
  a feather-edge margin that allows the clinician to position the crown margin after
  cementation, theoretically optimizing the biologic width. This 10-year prospective
  study on 33 patients (39 single-crown abutments, 52 FPD abutments) is the longest
  published follow-up for BOPT zirconia restorations. The periodontal outcomes are
  striking: gingival margin stability was maintained in over 90% of restorations,
  gingival thickness increased by ~0.45 mm, and plaque index remained below 1 throughout.
  Biological and mechanical complication rates were low and comparable between single
  crowns and FPDs. The untreated antagonist control group allows some internal benchmarking.
  Limitations include small sample size and single-center design. For a restorative
  student, this is the strongest long-term evidence yet that BOPT is a viable — and
  periodontal-friendly — preparation philosophy for zirconia.'
