  delivery by 48%, with further measures targeting urgent care capacity and long-term
  service sustainability. The UDA system remains deeply controversial — critics argue

  is a meaningful signal that targeted commissioning changes can move the needle quickly.
  For students and clinicians outside the UK, the story is a useful case study in
  how public dental systems respond to access crises: the levers are contractual and
  financial, not clinical. Whether the Isle of Wight gains translate to patient outcomes
  rather than just activity numbers is the question the data will eventually have
  to answer.'
