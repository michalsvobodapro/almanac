summaryDeep: 'Intraoral radiographic technique is a mandatory but conceptually dense
  component of dental undergraduate training, and students frequently struggle with
  angulation principles and film placement logic. This exploratory RCT randomised
  48 third-year BDS students to either ChatGPT-assisted learning (using standardised
  prompts) or conventional self-directed study following a standardised lecture. The
  ChatGPT group showed statistically significant intragroup improvements in several
  IRT components, particularly the bisecting angle technique and occlusal radiography,
  while the SDL group showed less consistent gains. Intergroup comparisons were significant
  for specific subtopics. The study''s main limitation is its size — 24 students per
  arm is insufficient to draw broad conclusions — and the use of theoretical knowledge
  tests only, without clinical skill assessment. Nevertheless, for a dental student
  in Prague navigating complex radiographic concepts, the practical implication is
  clear: using ChatGPT with structured prompts as a study supplement is a low-cost,
  evidence-nudged strategy worth trying.'
