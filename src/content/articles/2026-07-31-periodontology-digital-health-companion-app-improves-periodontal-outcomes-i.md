summaryDeep: 'This multi-centre RCT across seven German university dental centres
  randomised 194 adults with periodontitis to either standard systematic periodontal
  therapy or the same therapy augmented by a digital health companion app. The trial
  is notable for its design rigour — multi-centre, randomised, adequately powered
  — in a space usually dominated by pilot studies and observational data. The app
  arm demonstrated superior clinical outcomes, suggesting that structured digital
  engagement between appointments translates into real biological benefit, likely
  through improved adherence to oral hygiene and behavioural reinforcement. For a
  dental student in Prague, this matters because EU health systems are actively evaluating
  digital therapeutics for reimbursement; evidence like this accelerates that process.
  It also sets a benchmark: future app studies will need to match this design to be
  taken seriously. The finding does not mean every app works — it means this specific,
  structured companion intervention works as an adjunct, not a replacement, for professional
  care.'
