summaryDeep: 'This multicenter study recruited subjects across eight countries, reading
  digital panoramic radiographs for primary and post-treatment apical periodontitis.
  The prevalence of AP was high across all sites, consistent with global data showing
  that roughly half of root-filled teeth in population samples harbour periapical
  pathology. Variables associated with AP included tooth type, restoration quality,
  and root-filling length — the usual suspects. What makes this study useful is its
  scale and geographic breadth: it establishes a Latin American epidemiological baseline
  that has been largely absent from the literature, which has historically been dominated
  by European and North American cohorts. For clinical education, the finding that
  post-treatment AP is common reinforces that obturation quality and coronal seal
  are not optional refinements — they are the primary determinants of long-term success.
  The cross-sectional design cannot establish causality or track progression, but
  the sample size lends the prevalence estimates reasonable precision.'
