summaryDeep: 'The retreatment-versus-endodontic-microsurgery decision is one of the
  most consequential judgment calls in endodontics, and AI prognostic tools are increasingly
  marketed as decision support. This scoping review (PRISMA-ScR, PROBAST+AI, TRIPOD+AI)
  searched seven databases through February 2026 and found just 6 AI prognostic studies
  meeting eligibility — all relying on internal validation only, none reporting calibration,
  external validation, or clinical utility analyses. Crucially, no model generates
  treatment-conditional comparative predictions: they estimate outcomes for a single
  pathway, not the differential prognosis of retreatment versus surgery. The 11 clinical
  studies informing Track 2 decision domains reveal a rich set of variables (canal
  anatomy, periapical lesion size, restorability, access) that current AI models largely
  ignore. For students: AI tools in endo are real but immature — none should currently
  substitute for structured clinical reasoning. The review doubles as a roadmap for
  what the next generation of models needs to deliver.'
