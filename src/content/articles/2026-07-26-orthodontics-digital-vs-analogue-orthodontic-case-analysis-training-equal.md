  training group, using the web-based app Orthotrainer, or an analogue training group,

summaryDeep: 'This RCT from RWTH Aachen randomized undergraduate students to either
  a digital web app or conventional analogue case analysis with physical models and
  printed records. Baseline knowledge was comparable between groups. Both groups improved
  significantly across two training sessions separated by three weeks (β = 8.31, 95%
  CI 2.66–13.96), with no overall group difference in the final analogue exam. The
  exploratory subarea finding — analogue superiority in extraoral assessment (b =
  23.37, 95% CI 7.31–39.42) — is the most clinically interesting result. It suggests
  that three-dimensional spatial reasoning about facial form may not transfer as well
  from screen-based learning. The sample size (n = 28) is small and the study is single-center,
  so these findings should be treated as hypothesis-generating. For dental education
  planners, the message is nuanced: digital tools are not inferior overall, but they
  may need supplementation for specific spatial assessment skills.'
