summaryDeep: Missed accessory canals, particularly MB2 in maxillary molars and middle
  mesial canals in mandibular molars, remain a leading cause of endodontic failure.
  This PROSPERO-registered systematic review mapped the current AI literature on automated
  detection and segmentation of these structures from periapical and CBCT images.
  While included models demonstrated high sensitivity for MB2 detection in controlled
  datasets, heterogeneity in imaging protocols, ground-truth definitions, and validation
  strategies makes cross-study comparison unreliable. Most models were trained on
  fewer than a few hundred cases from single institutions, raising serious generalizability
  concerns. The review highlights that AI-assisted canal detection could meaningfully
  reduce missed anatomy in routine practice — a genuine clinical problem — but calls
  for multicenter prospective validation before any clinical deployment. For students
  learning endodontic anatomy, the takeaway is that AI is being trained on the same
  radiographic ambiguities that challenge human readers, and its current performance
  reflects those limitations.
