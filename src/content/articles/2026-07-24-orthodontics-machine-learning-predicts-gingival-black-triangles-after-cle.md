  and a recognised complication of clear aligner therapy, yet pre-treatment prediction
  has relied on clinical intuition. This retrospective study of 340 patients (238
  training, 102 validation) applied univariate analysis, LASSO regression, and three
  machine learning algorithms to identify independent predictors of post-aligner gingival
  embrasures. Random forest outperformed logistic regression and SVM on AUC, calibration,
  and decision curve analysis. SHAP values provided interpretability — identifying
  which of the seven core variables drove individual predictions. The model is not
  yet validated externally or prospectively, but the methodology is sound and the
  clinical utility is real: a pre-treatment risk score could flag patients who need
  periodontal augmentation or modified tooth movement planning before aligner therapy
  begins. The cross-specialty relevance (orthodontics meets periodontology) makes
  this particularly useful for students thinking about interdisciplinary treatment
