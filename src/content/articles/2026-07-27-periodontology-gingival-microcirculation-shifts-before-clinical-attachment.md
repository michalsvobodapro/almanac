summaryDeep: 'This cohort study characterized gingival microcirculation at the site
  level using imaging-based methods, correlating vascular parameters with standard
  probing data (BOP, PPD) and digital color metrics from intraoral scans — all in
  patients without clinical attachment loss. Microcirculatory changes tracked reliably
  with early inflammatory signs, suggesting the vasculature responds to bacterial
  challenge before the periodontium structurally degrades. The clinical appeal is
  obvious: an objective, non-invasive measure of subclinical inflammation could allow
  earlier intervention and more precise monitoring of treatment response. The study
  is observational and limited to early disease stages, so it cannot yet predict progression
  to attachment loss. But as intraoral scanners become ubiquitous, integrating color
  and vascular metrics into routine perio assessment is a realistic near-term development
  — particularly relevant for students learning to distinguish health from early gingivitis.'
