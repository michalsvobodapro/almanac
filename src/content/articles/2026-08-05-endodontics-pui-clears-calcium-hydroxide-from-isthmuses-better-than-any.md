  used during root canal treatment [conventional irrigation (CU), Irriflex (IF), Easy
  Clean (EC), and passive ultrasonic irrigation (PUI)] in removing calcium hydroxide–based
  and calcium silicate–based intracanal medications from the isthmus of mesial root
  canals in 3D-printed mandibular molar replicas. A mandibular molar with presence

summaryDeep: 'Sixty-four standardized translucent resin replicas of a mandibular molar
  with an isthmus in the mesial roots were filled with either Ultracal XS or Bio-C
  Temp and subjected to four irrigation protocols after 7-day medication placement.
  Micro-CT before and after irrigation quantified residual medication volume. PUI
  achieved 98.2 ± 2.28% removal of Ultracal XS, significantly outperforming the other
  three techniques (p < 0.05); no significant differences existed between conventional
  irrigation, Irriflex, and Easy Clean for that material. For Bio-C Temp, all protocols
  performed similarly — suggesting the calcium silicate material''s physical properties
  make it easier to remove regardless of technique. The in-vitro design using resin
  replicas rather than extracted teeth is a limitation, as dentinal tubule interaction
  and organic debris are absent. Still, the standardization enabled by 3D-printed
  replicas is a methodological strength. Practically: if you''re using calcium hydroxide
  as interappointment medication, PUI is the irrigation upgrade that matters most
  for isthmus areas.'
