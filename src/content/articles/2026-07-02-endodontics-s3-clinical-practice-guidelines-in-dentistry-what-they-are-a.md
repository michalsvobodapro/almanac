summary: S3 guidelines — the highest tier of evidence-based clinical guidance in the

summaryDeep: 'The S3 designation signals a guideline developed through systematic
  evidence review combined with formal consensus methodology — a step above expert-opinion
  S1 and evidence-informed S2 documents. As European dental societies (including ESE
  for endodontics) increasingly adopt this framework, understanding what an S3 recommendation
  actually means becomes clinically relevant: a strong S3 recommendation carries a
  different weight than a consensus statement from a working group. This commentary
  in the International Endodontic Journal unpacks the structure — PICO questions,
  systematic searches, GRADE-style evidence grading, Delphi or nominal group consensus
  — and explains where S3 guidelines currently exist in dentistry and where gaps remain.
  For a Prague dental student, this is directly relevant: ČSK and Czech postgraduate
  education are increasingly aligned with European guideline frameworks, and knowing
  how to read and apply an S3 document is a core competency. No clinical protocol
  changes here, but essential methodological literacy.'
