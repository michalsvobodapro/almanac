  are particularly susceptible to recession and inflammation. Injectable platelet-rich
  fibrin (i-PRF) and microneedling (MN) are emerging minimally invasive approaches
  for enhancing gingival thickness and improving periodontal health. To evaluate the
  efficacy of i-PRF and MN combination compared to i-PRF or MN monotherapy in the
  treatment of thin gingival phenotype. A comprehensive literature search was conducted
  across five databases with no language restrictions. Studies were selected based
  on predefined selection criteria that assessed primary outcomes (Gingival Thickness
  - GT, Width of Keratinised Tissue - KTW). Four split-mouth, prospective randomized
  trials involving 116 participants were included. Data from these studies were extracted
  individually by two reviewers, with a risk of bias assessment performed using the

  found that combining injectable platelet-rich fibrin with microneedling produced
  significantly greater gingival thickness gains than i-PRF alone, particularly within

summaryDeep: 'Thin gingival phenotype is a recognised risk factor for recession and
  implant complications, and minimally invasive augmentation options are in high demand.
  This meta-analysis pools four split-mouth prospective RCTs — the strongest available
  design for intra-patient comparisons — and finds a clear short-term advantage for
  the i-PRF plus microneedling combination over i-PRF monotherapy in gingival thickness.
  The effect is most pronounced in the first three months, raising the question of
  whether it persists long-term; the review acknowledges that follow-up across included
  studies was limited. Keratinised tissue width showed no significant difference,
  which matters for treatment planning: if the goal is width rather than thickness,
  the combination adds little. Secondary outcomes (CAL, PD, plaque index) favoured
  the combination. The Cochrane RoB 2 assessment and RevMan analysis lend methodological
  credibility. For students learning periodontal augmentation, this is a useful evidence
  summary for a technique gaining traction in European practices.'
