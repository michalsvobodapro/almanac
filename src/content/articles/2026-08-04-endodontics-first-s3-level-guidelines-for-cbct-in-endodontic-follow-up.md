summaryDeep: 'CBCT has proliferated in endodontics without a robust, tiered evidence
  framework to govern its use in post-treatment surveillance. These S3-level guidelines
  fill that gap: developed through systematic evidence synthesis and structured expert
  consensus, they provide specific recommendations on indications, timing, and interpretation
  of CBCT in follow-up scenarios — including when periapical radiographs remain sufficient
  and when 3D imaging adds diagnostic value that changes management. S3 status means
  the recommendations carry the highest methodological weight in European guideline
  hierarchies, comparable to GRADE-informed clinical practice guidelines elsewhere.
  For students and practitioners in the Czech Republic, where CBCT availability is
  expanding rapidly, this document will likely become the reference standard for justifying
  or withholding 3D imaging in endo review appointments. It also has direct relevance
  to radiation protection obligations under EU EURATOM-derived legislation, which
  requires justification of every ionising exposure.'
