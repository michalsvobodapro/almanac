summaryDeep: 'Forty-five extracted single-rooted premolars were obturated, then retreated
  with one of three rotary NiTi systems: ProTaper Universal Retreatment, EndoStar
  RE-Endo, or Retreaty. Roots were sectioned at 3, 6, and 9 mm from the apex and examined
  under stereomicroscope for dentinal defects and file deformation. ProTaper Universal
  showed the highest frequency of mechanical failure (fracture and deformation); Retreaty
  had the lowest incidence of dentinal cracks. Statistical analysis used one-way ANOVA
  and chi-square. The study is in vitro and uses extracted teeth, so clinical extrapolation

  real-world performance. That said, the pattern is consistent with prior literature:
