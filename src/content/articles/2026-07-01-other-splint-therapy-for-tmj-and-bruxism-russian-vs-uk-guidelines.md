summaryDeep: 'Splint therapy is one of the most commonly prescribed interventions
  for temporomandibular disorders and bruxism, yet clinical guidelines across countries
  diverge substantially. This comparative study examines the 2022 Russian guideline
  (''Pain Dysfunction Syndrome of the TMJ / Costen''s Syndrome'') against the 2025
  UK guideline (''Management of Painful Temporomandibular Disorder in Adults''). Key
  divergences include: the UK guideline prioritizes reversible, conservative approaches
  (stabilization splints, physiotherapy, cognitive behavioral therapy) before any
  occlusal intervention, while the Russian guideline places greater emphasis on occlusal
  correction and a wider range of splint designs earlier in the treatment sequence.
  The UK 2025 document also integrates more recent RCT evidence on the limited efficacy
  of repositioning splints for long-term pain management. For a Czech dental student,
  this is relevant because ČSK guidelines on TMD are closer to the Western European
  consensus — understanding where the evidence base is strong (stabilization splints
  for pain reduction) versus weak (repositioning splints for structural change) is
  essential for evidence-based practice.'
