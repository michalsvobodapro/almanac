summaryDeep: 'This PRISMA 2020-registered systematic review and meta-analysis is the
  most comprehensive synthesis yet of Nd:YAG laser as an adjunct to SRP, drawing on
  22 RCTs published 2010–2025 (n = 776). The headline finding — a statistically significant
  0.46 mm additional pocket depth reduction over SRP alone — is real but clinically
  modest; the confidence interval barely clears zero. Subgroup analysis reveals that
  split-mouth RCTs inflate the effect, likely due to within-patient controls reducing
  biological noise. Bleeding on probing, clinical attachment level, and plaque index
  were also assessed, with GRADE used to rate certainty. The practical upshot: Nd:YAG
  adds a measurable but small benefit, and the decision to invest in the technology
  should weigh cost and patient selection carefully. It does not replace meticulous
  SRP; it nudges outcomes at the margin.'
