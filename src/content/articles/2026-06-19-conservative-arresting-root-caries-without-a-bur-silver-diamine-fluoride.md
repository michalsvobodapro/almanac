summaryDeep: 'This systematic review with network meta-analysis searched five databases
  and included 17 RCTs evaluating nonrestorative strategies for root caries arrest.
  SUCRA rankings placed quarterly 5% NaF varnish, NaF varnish combined with CPP-ACP,
  40% CHX varnish, and annual 38% SDF as the professionally-applied agents superior
  to positive control. SDF''s annual application schedule is a practical advantage
  in patients with limited access to care. Risk of bias was low in only two studies;
  thirteen raised concerns, and CINeMA certainty was accordingly modest. Self-applied
  strategies did not reach statistical superiority over controls in the network. For
  a Prague dental student treating elderly or medically compromised patients in clinic,
  this review provides a ranked, evidence-based menu: reach for NaF varnish quarterly
  as the default, reserve SDF for patients where aesthetics are less critical or access
  is constrained. The absence of restorative intervention as a comparator is a limitation
  — the review does not answer whether arresting beats restoring, only which arrest
  strategy works best.'
