summaryDeep: 'This parallel-arm RCT compared four regimens — green tea extract floss
  (GTE-DF), chlorhexidine floss (CHX-DF), plain waxed floss, and toothbrushing only
  — over 15 days in healthy young adults following baseline scaling. Both medicated
  flosses outperformed plain floss and brushing alone for plaque reduction. The key
  finding: GTE-DF and CHX-DF were statistically equivalent for plaque (Turesky-modified
  Quigley-Hein index), but CHX-DF was significantly superior for gingival bleeding
  reduction at both timepoints. The study is short (15 days), small (n=15 per group),
  and conducted in a healthy population — generalisation to periodontitis patients
  is limited. Still, for patients who refuse CHX due to staining or taste, green tea
  floss is a credible alternative for plaque control, with the caveat that bleeding
  management may require additional intervention.'
