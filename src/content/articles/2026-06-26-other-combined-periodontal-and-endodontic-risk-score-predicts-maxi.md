summaryDeep: 'Maxillary sinus membrane thickening is a frequent incidental CBCT finding,
  and its odontogenic origins are often underappreciated. This study of 1,056 dentate
  maxillary sinuses from 562 patients builds a composite risk score — the COS — by
  weighting periodontal bone loss severity, periapical lesion presence, and endodontic
  treatment history using multivariable logistic regression with GEE. Internal validation
  via 1,000-resample bootstrapping confirms stability. The AUC improvement from 0.602
  (bone loss alone) to 0.718 (COS) is modest but meaningful: it means the combined
  score correctly reclassifies a meaningful proportion of sinuses. For implant planning
  in the posterior maxilla, this is directly relevant — a patient with both periodontal
  disease and periapical pathology in the upper posterior quadrant carries a substantially
  higher sinus disease burden than bone loss alone would suggest. The COS needs external
  validation before clinical adoption, but the conceptual framework — treating the
  posterior maxilla as a shared perio-endo-sinus ecosystem — is immediately useful
  for treatment planning conversations.'
