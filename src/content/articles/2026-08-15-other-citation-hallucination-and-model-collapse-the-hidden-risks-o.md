  references — is particularly dangerous in a field where a student or clinician might
  accept an AI-generated literature summary without cross-checking PubMed. Model collapse
  describes the degradation that occurs when AI systems are iteratively trained on
  AI-generated outputs rather than human-verified data, creating a feedback loop of
  compounding inaccuracy. The framework proposed for clinical decision-making emphasises
  that AI outputs must be treated as a first draft requiring expert verification,
