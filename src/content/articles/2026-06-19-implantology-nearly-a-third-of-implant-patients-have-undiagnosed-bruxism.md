  complications. This cross-sectional study aimed to assess the presence and type

  presenting for implant treatment and applied both clinical examination and validated
  self-report questionnaires (Bruxism Questionnaire, Fonseca Anamnestic Index, PSS-14)
  using the 2025 international consensus criteria. Clinical diagnosis identified bruxism
  in 31.3% of participants; self-report captured 26% — a meaningful gap that underscores
  reliance on patient history alone is insufficient. Bruxism was significantly associated
