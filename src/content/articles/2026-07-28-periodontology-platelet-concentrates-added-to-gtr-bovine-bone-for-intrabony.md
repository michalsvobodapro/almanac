summaryDeep: 'Platelet concentrates — PRP, PRF, and their variants — have been marketed
  as regenerative boosters in periodontal surgery for years, but the evidence base
  has remained murky. This systematic review focused specifically on the combination
  of APCs with guided tissue regeneration and bovine-derived bone grafts in intrabony
  defects deeper than 3 mm in systemically healthy adults. Five RCTs met inclusion
  criteria from an initial pool of 214 articles. Four showed no statistically significant
  benefit from adding APCs; one reported potential gains. Critically, all five studies
  had risk-of-bias concerns, primarily from absent predefined protocols — a methodological
  weakness that inflates uncertainty. The review does not support routine addition
  of platelet concentrates to this already complex regenerative protocol. For a student
  watching clinicians enthusiastically spin blood in centrifuges, this is a useful
  reality check: the biological rationale is sound, but the clinical payoff in this
  specific combination remains unproven. Better-designed trials with standardized
  APC preparation protocols are needed before practice should change.'
