summaryDeep: 'Pooling seven trials that contributed to the primary caries-arrest outcome,
  this Quintessence meta-analysis found SDF and conventional restorations (ART, GIC-based)
  performed comparably — the risk ratio of 1.25 crossed unity and confidence intervals
  were wide. The elephant in the room is I²=96%: the studies are measuring different
  things in different ways, making the pooled estimate fragile. Sensitivity analyses
  and subgroup work were prespecified but could not resolve the heterogeneity. What
  this means clinically: SDF remains a legitimate, evidence-supported option for caries
  arrest in primary teeth, particularly where conventional treatment is impractical
  (behaviour management issues, resource-limited settings), but it should not be presented
  to parents as proven equivalent — the data simply aren''t clean enough yet. The
  tooth-staining barrier identified in the Libyan survey (also in today''s digest)
  is real and should be part of the consent conversation.'
