summaryDeep: 'As LLMs enter clinical decision-support, knowing which model to trust
  — and how much — is a practical question. This study constructed 25 guideline-aligned
  pediatric caries-risk scenarios and had six expert raters score five chatbot responses
  on Likert scales for accuracy, completeness, relevance, clarity, and usefulness.
  ANOVA with Bonferroni correction confirmed significant between-model differences
  on every criterion. ChatGPT-5 and Claude 4.5 Sonnet consistently outperformed the
  open-source alternatives; Mistral 7B lagged on accuracy and completeness. Readability
  analysis showed frontier models also produced more accessible prose. Limitations:
  expert-rated Likert scales are subjective, the 25-question set may not cover the
  full clinical space, and model versions update rapidly. Still, this is the most
  systematic head-to-head on this specific task to date. The finding that even the
  best models showed gaps in completeness is a caution against uncritical use at the
  chair.'
