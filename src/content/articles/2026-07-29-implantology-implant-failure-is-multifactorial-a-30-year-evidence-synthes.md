summaryDeep: 'The review included RCTs, cohort studies, and prior systematic reviews
  with at least one year of follow-up, covering patient-related, surgical, prosthetic,
  implant-related, and peri-implant biological factors. The strength of the evidence
  is moderate overall, with the clearest signals around smoking and uncontrolled diabetes
  — both of which impair osseointegration through overlapping vascular and immune
  mechanisms. History of periodontitis emerges as an independent risk factor even
  after tooth loss, reinforcing the concept of a susceptible host phenotype. Practically,
  the review underscores that pre-implant risk stratification should be systematic
  rather than intuitive: a structured checklist covering glycaemic control, smoking
  status, residual periodontal disease, and bone density assessment changes the conversation
  before the first implant is placed.'
