summaryDeep: 'COSET was published to standardize outcome reporting across endodontic
  clinical research, covering eight domains from pain and radiographic healing to
  tooth survival and patient satisfaction. This PROSPERO-registered bibliometric review
  compared 26 pre-publication studies (2022–2023) with 37 post-publication studies
  (2024–2025). Overall adherence barely moved and actually dipped. Radiographic healing
  was universally reported — unsurprisingly, since it is easy to measure — but pain
  reporting fell to 73% and signs of infection to 70%. The patient-centred domains
  that arguably matter most to patients were near-absent. The finding is a direct
  challenge to the endodontic research community and has implications for how students
  should critically appraise the literature: a study reporting only radiographic healing
  tells you far less than it appears to.'
