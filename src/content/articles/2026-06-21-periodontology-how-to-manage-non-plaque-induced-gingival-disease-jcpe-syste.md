summaryDeep: 'Non-plaque-induced gingival diseases are a heterogeneous group that
  routinely stumps general practitioners — and this systematic review from JCPE is
  the most comprehensive synthesis of their management to date. Part 2 addresses three
  clinically distinct domains: inflammatory and immune-mediated conditions (including
  mucous membrane pemphigoid, lichen planus, and lupus-related lesions), gingival
  neoplasms (benign and malignant), and gingival pigmentation disorders. For each,
  the review maps current evidence onto diagnostic criteria and treatment options,
  highlighting where evidence is robust and where clinical decision-making still relies
  on expert consensus. The immune-mediated conditions section is particularly valuable:
  it clarifies when topical corticosteroids suffice, when systemic immunosuppression
  is warranted, and when urgent biopsy is non-negotiable. For students and general
  practitioners, this review functions as a decision-support framework for presentations
  that don''t respond to scaling — a scenario that should immediately trigger NPIGD
  workup.'
