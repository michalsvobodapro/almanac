  consumption-a Group 1 carcinogen affecting 600 million people globally-represents
  a significant unmet need in precision prevention. Despite the known role of COX-2-mediated

  Methods We emulated a target trial using a nationwide matched cohort of 50,606 areca
  nut chewers from the Taiwan National Health Insurance Research Database (2008 to
  2021). To minimize selection and immortal time biases, we utilized time-dependent
  exposure modeling and Fine-Gray subdistribution hazard models to account for competing
  mortality. Results Aspirin initiation was associated with a significantly lower

  findings provide large-scale human evidence that sustained aspirin use significantly
  alters the trajectory of oral field cancerization in areca nut chewers. This study
  identifies aspirin as a high-priority candidate for risk-stratified chemoprevention
  in global populations with high areca nut exposure.

  emulation used time-dependent exposure modelling and Fine-Gray subdistribution hazard
  models to handle competing mortality — methodologically more rigorous than a simple
  cohort comparison. The adjusted subdistribution hazard ratio of 0.75 (95% CI 0.65–0.87)

  cannot establish causation, the scale (n = 50,606, 13-year follow-up) and methodological
  care make it the strongest human signal yet. It won't change practice tomorrow —
