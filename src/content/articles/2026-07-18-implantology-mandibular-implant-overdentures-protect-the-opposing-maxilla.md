summaryDeep: 'Maxillary residual ridge resorption is an underappreciated long-term
  consequence of mandibular prosthetic design in fully edentulous patients. This systematic
  review synthesized 18 studies — RCTs, prospective and retrospective designs — with
  GRADE assessment, finding that mandibular implant-supported overdentures (IODs)
  generally produce less maxillary RRR than conventional complete dentures, regardless
  of implant number. The more clinically actionable finding is the attachment system
  effect: resilient non-splinting systems (O-ring, ball, locator, magnet) were consistently
  associated with less maxillary RRR than rigid bar-retained designs. The proposed
  mechanism is biomechanical — rigid splinting transmits higher occlusal forces to
  the opposing ridge. Evidence certainty is moderate due to heterogeneity in study
  designs and RRR measurement methods, so this should inform rather than dictate prosthetic
  planning. For students: when planning mandibular IODs, the attachment choice has
  consequences beyond the mandible.'
