summaryDeep: 'Pathological tooth migration in periodontally compromised patients is
  a common and challenging presentation, and the evidence base for combined treatment
  has been thin. This 18-month longitudinal study treated 30 systemically healthy
  adults with initial periodontal therapy followed by fixed orthodontics using light
  forces, with selective intrusion where indicated. Clinical attachment level, probing
  depth, gingival recession, and tooth mobility were recorded at baseline, post-treatment,
  and follow-up. All key periodontal parameters improved significantly, and outcomes
  were stable at 18 months — a meaningful follow-up window for this patient group.
  The study is small and non-randomised, but the clinical protocol it describes (thorough
  perio stabilisation before any orthodontic loading, light forces throughout, intrusion
  as a targeted tool) aligns with EFP guidance on treating periodontitis patients
  orthodontically. For a student, the key lesson is sequencing: attempting orthodontic
  correction in an uncontrolled periodontal environment accelerates bone loss.'
