summaryDeep: 'This single-blind RCT enrolled 120 adults treated with 40% H₂O₂ in-office
  bleaching and randomized them across six dentifrice groups (n=20 each): conventional
  control plus five whitening formulations spanning abrasive, optical, and chemical
  whitening mechanisms, one of which also contained potassium nitrate. Spectrophotometric
  CIELAB measurements at baseline, immediately post-bleach, 1 week, 1 month, and 3
  months showed no significant between-group differences in ΔE*ab at any time point.
  A significant time × group interaction emerged primarily because the control group
  exhibited modest color regression at 3 months — not because whitening dentifrices
  produced superior whitening. Sensitivity peaked in the first 48 hours across all
  groups; the potassium nitrate formulation trended toward lower scores but did not
  reach significance after Bonferroni correction. The practical upshot: premium whitening
  dentifrices do not extend bleaching results, but a desensitizing formulation is
  a reasonable recommendation for sensitive patients post-bleach.'
