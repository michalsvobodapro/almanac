summaryDeep: 'Advanced platelet-rich fibrin (A-PRF) has been promoted as a biologic
  alternative to corticosteroids for managing post-extraction morbidity, but head-to-head
  RCT data were sparse. This split-mouth design is methodologically strong — each
  patient serves as their own control, eliminating inter-patient variability. Oral
  dexamethasone outperformed A-PRF on pain (VAS at all 13 measurement points), facial
  edema (days 2, 7, 14, 30), and trismus (maximum interincisal distance). Analgesic
  consumption was significantly lower on the dexamethasone side. No significant difference
  was found between sides for dry socket incidence. The result does not invalidate
  A-PRF — it may still offer wound-healing benefits beyond pain control — but it firmly
  repositions dexamethasone as the more effective pharmacological choice for acute
  postoperative symptom management. For students managing third molar extractions:
  the steroid protocol is the evidence-based default unless contraindicated.'
