summaryDeep: 'Researchers searched four major databases for studies on immunomodulatory
  therapies in periodontal disease, including cell lines, animal models, and human
  trials. Thirteen agent classes were evaluated — ranging from resolvins and statins
  to biologics — across 42 included studies. Several agents reduced alveolar bone
  loss and inflammatory markers in preclinical models, and limited human data suggest
  benefit in specific comorbidity contexts (e.g., diabetes, rheumatoid arthritis).
  However, evidence quality and consistency varied widely, safety data in the periodontal
  context are scarce, and no agent has been tested in adequately powered RCTs against
  conventional PMPR alone. The review is honest about these limitations and calls
  for standardized trial designs. For a student, this is a useful map of where the
  field is heading: host modulation therapy is the conceptual frontier of periodontology,
  but it remains a research agenda rather than a clinical toolkit. Watch for phase
  II/III trials over the next five years.'
