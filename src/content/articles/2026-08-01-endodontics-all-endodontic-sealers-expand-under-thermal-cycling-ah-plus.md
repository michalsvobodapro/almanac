summaryDeep: 'Thermal cycling (9,000 cycles, 5–55°C) was used to simulate aging in
  40 extracted single-rooted teeth obturated with one of four sealers using the single-cone
  technique: AH Plus, MTA Fillapex, TotalFill BC Sealer, or EndoREZ. Pre- and post-cycling
  micro-CT scans quantified volumetric changes at the apical 1 mm, apical 2 mm, and
  total canal volume. AH Plus showed the greatest expansion at the apical 1 mm (0.016
  mm³), significantly exceeding TotalFill BC (0.001 mm³), MTA Fillapex (0.003 mm³),
  and EndoREZ (0.008 mm³). At the apical 2 mm, only TotalFill BC differed significantly
  from AH Plus. The clinical significance of these micro-scale volumetric changes
  is uncertain — expansion could theoretically improve apical seal or generate stress
  on dentinal walls. This is an in-vitro study and thermal cycling is an imperfect
  aging proxy. AH Plus remains the most clinically validated sealer overall; this
  data adds nuance but doesn''t overturn its status.'
