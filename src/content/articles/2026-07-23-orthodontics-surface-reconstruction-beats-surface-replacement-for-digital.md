summaryDeep: 'Digital retainer fabrication is now mainstream, but the virtual bracket
  removal step — necessary when post-treatment scans are taken before debonding —
  introduces error that propagates through to the final appliance. This study is the
  first to compare RC and RP workflows across digital models, printed models, and
  vacuum-formed retainers using RMS deviation against the ground-truth post-treatment
  model. RC was more consistent overall; RP''s initial advantage for posterior teeth
  was lost after 3D printing, suggesting the printing step is a significant error
  source that workflow choice cannot fully compensate for. The sample of 14 paired
  models is small, limiting statistical power. Clinically, both workflows are acceptable
  — but if your lab or software offers both, RC is the safer default. For students
  entering a digitally integrated curriculum: understanding where error accumulates
  in the digital chain (scan → VBR → print → thermoform) is increasingly a core competency.'
