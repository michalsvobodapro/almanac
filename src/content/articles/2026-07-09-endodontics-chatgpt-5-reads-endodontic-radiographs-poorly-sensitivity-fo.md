  are increasingly used for dental radiograph interpretation, especially among patients
  seeking self-assessment. However, their diagnostic accuracy in endodontics remains
  unclear. Methods This cross-sectional STARD-AI-compliant study analyzed 271 anonymized
  periapical radiographs of endodontically treated posterior teeth, classified as
  straightforward (n = 167) or complex (n = 104), using standardized ChatGPT-5 prompts.
  Diagnostic criteria included obturation length (short, adequate, long), presence

summaryDeep: 'The study is methodologically rigorous: 271 anonymized radiographs of
  endodontically treated posterior teeth, split into straightforward and complex cases,
  evaluated with standardized ChatGPT-5 prompts and benchmarked against a specialist
  reference standard and a panel of general dentists. ChatGPT-5''s near-perfect specificity
  means it rarely raises false alarms on healthy teeth — but its sensitivity collapses
  when pathology is present. For periapical lesions specifically, sensitivity ranged
  from 10.5% to 28.6%, meaning the model misses roughly 7 in 10 lesions. General dentists,
  despite their own limitations, outperformed the AI on every accuracy metric. The
  study is cross-sectional and limited to text-prompt interaction (no fine-tuned dental
  imaging model), but the STARD-AI compliance makes the methodology sound. The practical
  message is unambiguous: AI chatbots are not diagnostic tools for endodontic radiograph
  interpretation and should not be used as such by patients or clinicians.'
