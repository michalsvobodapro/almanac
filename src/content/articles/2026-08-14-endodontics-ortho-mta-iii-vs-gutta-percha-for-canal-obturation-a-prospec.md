summary: A prospective RCT pits Ortho MTA III — an injectable calcium-silicate-based
  material — against continuous-wave gutta-percha with AH Plus sealer for orthograde
  obturation. High-grade evidence from a randomised design makes this one of the cleaner
  head-to-heads the field has seen. Prognostic factors associated with outcome differences
  are also identified, adding clinical depth beyond a simple win/loss verdict.
summaryDeep: Orthograde obturation with bioceramic materials has been gaining ground
  on gutta-percha, but prospective randomised data have been thin. This trial fills
  that gap by directly comparing Ortho MTA III (OMTA) — placed without a separate
  sealer — against the continuous wave of compaction technique using gutta-percha
  and AH Plus, the current clinical reference standard. The randomised prospective
  design earns a high evidence grade, making the findings unusually actionable. Beyond
  the primary outcome comparison, the study maps predictive factors — likely including
  preoperative periapical status, tooth type, and pulp vitality — that modulate success
  rates in each arm. For students and clinicians weighing the premium cost of bioceramic
  systems against proven GP protocols, this trial provides the kind of structured
  evidence that should anchor the decision rather than anecdote or manufacturer data.
  Full results are behind the journal paywall, but the design alone signals this is
  a reference paper for the next iteration of obturation guidelines.
