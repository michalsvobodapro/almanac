summaryDeep: 'This parallel-group, double-blind RCT allocated 60 participants with
  dentin hypersensitivity (DH) to four arms: laser alone, 8% arginine toothpaste alone,
  combined laser plus arginine, and control. Assessments at baseline, 2, 4, and 8
  weeks used evaporative (air) and tactile stimuli with VAS and Schiff scores. Mean
  Schiff scores fell from 5.0 at baseline to 1.9 at 8 weeks across active groups —
  a clinically meaningful reduction. The control group also improved, suggesting some
  natural desensitization, but active groups outperformed it. The null finding between
  active interventions is the key clinical message: there is no additive benefit to
  combining PBM with arginine. Given that laser therapy carries additional cost and
  equipment requirements, arginine toothpaste alone appears the most cost-effective
  first-line approach. Clinicians should not be upselling combination therapy on the
  basis of synergy — the evidence doesn''t support it.'
