summary: The Dent.DocCard is a printable, laminated reference card (99×204 mm) that
  guides dental professionals through court-admissible documentation of injuries in
  domestic violence cases, including a 1:1 scale ruler for injury measurement. Dentists
  are often the first clinicians to see orofacial trauma — this card operationalizes
  that responsibility.
summaryDeep: 'Orofacial injuries account for a disproportionate share of domestic
  violence trauma, yet dental teams rarely receive structured training in forensic
  documentation. The Dent.DocCard addresses this gap with a format designed for chairside
  use: it summarizes what to document, how to describe injuries in legally defensible
  language, and provides a physical scale for photographing wound dimensions at true
  size. The card is freely downloadable and designed to be laminated for clinical
  use. This is not a research paper — it is a practical educational tool — but its
  clinical relevance is high, particularly in a primary care dental setting where
  a patient may not present to emergency medicine. For Czech dental students, awareness
  of mandatory reporting obligations under Czech law (zákon o sociálně-právní ochraně
  dětí and related provisions) makes this kind of structured documentation protocol
  directly applicable. The tool''s simplicity is its strength: it removes the excuse
  of not knowing what to do.'
