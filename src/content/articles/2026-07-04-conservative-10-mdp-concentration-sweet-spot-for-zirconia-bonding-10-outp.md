summaryDeep: 'Nine groups of zirconia specimens (n=6 each) were treated with MDP solutions
  ranging from 2–14% v/v in ethanol, a commercial MDP primer (Z-Prime Plus), or no
  primer, then bonded with flowable composite and tested in shear. Bond strength rose
  from 7.1 MPa at 2% to a peak of 11.3 MPa at 10%, then fell to 7.6–7.7 MPa at 12–14%.
  ³¹P NMR spectroscopy revealed the mechanism: at optimal concentration, MDP forms
  bidentate coordination bonds with zirconia''s Zr-OH surface; above 10%, excess MDP
  molecules shift to weaker monodentate coordination and form multilayer aggregates
  that reduce bond quality. EDS confirmed zirconium-phosphate interaction at the interface.
  The sample size per group (n=6) is small, and in vitro shear bond testing has limited
  clinical predictive value, but the mechanistic explanation is unusually rigorous
  for this type of study. Practical implication: when using experimental or lab-prepared
  MDP primers, 10% is the target concentration.'
