summaryDeep: 'The FEA model was built from patient CT data and loaded with 500 N vertical
  and 250 N oblique masticatory forces — physiologically plausible values. The prebent
  plate showed the highest plate stress under vertical loading (135.6 MPa), while
  PSP showed slightly higher screw and bone stress but the lowest overall displacement,
  suggesting better load distribution across the fixation construct. Graft stress
  values were comparable across all systems. The limitation is inherent to FEA: a
  single anatomical model, no material fatigue modelling, and no in vivo validation.
  Still, for oral and maxillofacial surgeons planning large advancements where segment
  stability is critical to graft integration, this adds biomechanical rationale for
  PSP consideration — particularly as CAD/CAM costs continue to fall.'
