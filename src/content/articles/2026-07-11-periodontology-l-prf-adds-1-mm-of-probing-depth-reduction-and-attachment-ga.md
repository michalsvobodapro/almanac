summaryDeep: This PRISMA-compliant systematic review and meta-analysis synthesised
  23 RCTs evaluating leukocyte and platelet-rich fibrin (L-PRF) as an adjunct to open
  flap debridement (OFD) in periodontal intrabony defects. At the primary 9-month
  endpoint (16 studies), L-PRF + OFD produced a pooled mean probing depth reduction
  of 1.11 mm (95% CI 0.84–1.38) and clinical attachment level gain of 1.13 mm (95%
  CI 0.79–1.48) versus OFD alone — both statistically significant. Secondary outcomes
  including radiographic bone fill and gingival margin level also favoured the L-PRF
  group. Risk of bias was low in 12 studies, with 8 raising some concerns and 3 rated
  high risk. Subgroup analyses explored lesion morphology, centrifugation protocols,
  and application technique, though heterogeneity limits definitive conclusions there.
  The GRADE certainty of evidence spans very low to moderate, meaning the 1 mm gain
  is directionally reliable but the exact magnitude should be interpreted cautiously.
  For a student entering clinic, L-PRF is an accessible, autologous, low-cost adjunct
  that is now backed by the strongest evidence base it has ever had.
