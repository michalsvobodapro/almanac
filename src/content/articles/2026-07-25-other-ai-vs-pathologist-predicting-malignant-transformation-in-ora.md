  decision of how aggressively to monitor or treat a dysplastic lesion hinges on risk
  stratification. Conventional pathologist grading (mild/moderate/severe) has well-documented
  reproducibility problems. This systematic review evaluated AI-based risk prediction
  models — likely including deep-learning image analysis of histological slides —
  against pathologist assessment for predicting malignant transformation. The moderate
  GRADE confidence reflects the heterogeneity typical of AI diagnostic reviews: variable
  model architectures, small training sets, and inconsistent outcome definitions.
  What the review adds is a structured comparison of whether AI models outperform,
  match, or underperform experienced pathologists. For students, the clinical relevance
  is immediate: oral medicine rotations increasingly involve AI-assisted pathology,
  and understanding where these tools add value (reproducibility, throughput) versus
  where they remain limited (rare variants, clinical context) is essential literacy.'
