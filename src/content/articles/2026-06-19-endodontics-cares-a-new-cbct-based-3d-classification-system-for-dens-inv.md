summaryDeep: 'Dens invaginatus is a developmental anomaly with highly variable internal
  anatomy that the Oehlers classification — based on periapical radiographs — cannot
  fully capture. CARES was developed by an expert group using CBCT imaging to create
  a comprehensive three-dimensional coding system that characterizes the invagination''s
  extent, communication with the pulp, and periapical status in a standardized, reproducible
  format. The system is designed for both clinical treatment planning and research
  standardization. For a student encountering DI in clinic — often presenting as a
  young patient with a non-vital upper lateral incisor — CBCT-guided classification
  directly informs whether conventional root canal treatment, surgical intervention,
  or intentional replantation is indicated. The Oehlers system will remain in use
  for a while, but CARES represents the direction of travel: 3D anatomy demands 3D
  classification. This is a consensus/classification paper rather than a clinical
  trial, so it carries no direct GRADE evidence rating, but its clinical utility is
  immediate.'
