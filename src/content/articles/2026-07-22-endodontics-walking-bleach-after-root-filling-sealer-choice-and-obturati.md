excerpt: The present study aimed to evaluate the effect of different root canal sealers
  and obturation strategies on colour stability and fracture resistance of maxillary
  incisors following a staining and walking bleaching protocol. One hundred and twenty
  human maxillary incisors were prepared and allocated to six groups (n = 15) according
  to sealer type (AH Plus or MTA Cem S), presence of gutta-percha, and application
  of staining/bleaching procedures. Two additional groups included teeth that underwent
  staining/ bleaching without endodontic treatment and intact teeth. Colour was measured
  spectrophotometrically in the CIE Lab* colour space at serial time points, and CIEDE2000
  (ΔE00) values were calculated for interval-specific and cumulative colour change.
  Fracture resistance was measured in Newtons using a universal testing machine, and
  fracture patterns were classified as repairable or non-repairable. Colour-coordinate
  data were analysed using linear mixed-effects models. ΔE00 values and fracture resistance
  were compared using one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey HSD test. In the six experimental
  root-filled groups, a 2 × 3 factorial ANOVA was additionally performed to examine
  the interaction between bleaching status and filling strategy. Repairability was
  analysed using the Pearson chi-square test. Significant within-group changes in
  L* and a* values were observed across all experimental conditions, whereas significant
  changes in b* values were detected in the staining/bleaching groups-

summary: An in-vitro study of 120 maxillary incisors found that sealer type (AH Plus
  vs MTA Cem S) and presence of gutta-percha significantly influenced both colour
  change and fracture resistance following staining and walking bleach. MTA-based
  sealer groups showed greater discolouration susceptibility. Fracture patterns differed
  by filling strategy, with clinical implications for tooth restorability.
summaryDeep: Six experimental groups varied sealer type, presence of gutta-percha,
  and staining/bleaching status; two additional groups (staining/bleaching without
  root filling, intact teeth) served as controls. Colour was measured spectrophotometrically
  at serial time points using CIEDE2000 (ΔE00). The interaction between bleaching
  status and filling strategy was significant in factorial ANOVA, meaning the effect
  of bleaching on colour depends on what is inside the canal — not a trivial finding
  for clinicians planning internal bleaching. MTA Cem S groups showed larger cumulative
  colour change, which may reflect the known discolouration potential of MTA-based
  materials when exposed to bleaching agents. Fracture resistance was influenced by
  obturation strategy, and fracture patterns (repairable vs non-repairable) differed
  by group — a clinically critical distinction. The study is in-vitro with bovine-derived
  standardisation limitations, but the factorial design is rigorous and the findings
  are directly applicable to treatment planning for discoloured endodontically treated
  incisors.
