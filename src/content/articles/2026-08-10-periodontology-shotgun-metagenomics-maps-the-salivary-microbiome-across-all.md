summaryDeep: Published in the Journal of Clinical Periodontology, this cross-sectional
  study applied shotgun metagenomic sequencing to saliva from 204 adults stratified
  by periodontal status, capturing taxonomic and functional microbiome profiles across
  health, gingivitis, and periodontitis stages. Unlike 16S rRNA amplicon studies,
  shotgun sequencing resolves species-level taxonomy and functional gene content simultaneously,
  and the multi-kingdom approach captures fungi and viruses alongside bacteria — a
  significant methodological advance. Stage-specific alterations in both community
  composition and metabolic pathways were identified, suggesting that functional dysbiosis
  (not just taxonomic shifts) tracks disease progression. As a cross-sectional design,
  causality cannot be established, and saliva is a proxy for subgingival communities.
  Still, this dataset provides the kind of high-resolution reference map that future
  diagnostic tools will need to validate against. Paired with the precision periodontology
  review above, it illustrates both the promise and the distance still to travel.
