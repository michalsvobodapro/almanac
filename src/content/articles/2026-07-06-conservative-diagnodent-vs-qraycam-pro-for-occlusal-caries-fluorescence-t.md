summaryDeep: This cross-sectional diagnostic accuracy study enrolled 64 adults contributing
  145 unrestored posterior teeth, using the tooth as the analytical unit. After prophylaxis
  and air-drying, calibrated examiners assigned ICDAS II scores, recorded DIAGNOdent
  Pen readings, and captured QrayCam Pro images. DIAGNOdent median values climbed
  from 10.5 (sound) to 74.0 (D1 dentinal lesion); QrayCam ΔFmax dropped from 0% to
  −60% across the same range. Spearman correlations were strong for DIAGNOdent (ρ=0.91)
  and moderate-to-strong for QrayCam ΔFmax (ρ=−0.80) and ΔRmax (ρ=0.67). ROC analyses
  confirmed reasonable discriminative ability. The study is exploratory and cross-sectional
  — no histological validation — so exact cut-off values should be treated cautiously.
  Practically, both devices can support ICDAS II in busy clinical or screening settings,
  particularly for monitoring borderline lesions over time without radiographic exposure.
