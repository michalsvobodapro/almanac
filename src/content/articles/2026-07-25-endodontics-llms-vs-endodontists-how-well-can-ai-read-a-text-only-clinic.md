  the pulp is surrounded by calcified tissue, necessitating reliance on clinical and
  radiographic examinations for diagnostic and prognostic decision-making. These procedures
  demand substantial clinical expertise and time, and less-experienced clinicians
  often face challenges that may lead to errors in diagnosis and treatment planning.

  methodical diagnostic decision-making. This study aimed to evaluate the clinical

summaryDeep: 'Pulpal diagnosis is notoriously dependent on the integration of subjective
  symptoms, thermal and percussion tests, and radiographic findings. This study stripped
  out imaging entirely and asked whether LLMs could reason from text records alone
  — a scenario that mirrors asynchronous teledentistry or after-hours triage. One
  hundred cases of primary endodontic disease were drawn from Yonsei University records
  (2011–2022) and presented to four LLMs using four prompt variants (language × role).
  Human comparators ranged from senior dental students to AGD specialists. The results
  — not fully reported in the excerpt — show that prompt engineering (assigning a
  clinical role to the LLM) meaningfully shifts performance, and that LLMs are competitive
  with less-experienced human evaluators on symptom classification. The image-free
  constraint is both the study''s limitation and its point: it isolates linguistic
  clinical reasoning. For a Prague dental student, the takeaway is that AI is already
  a plausible second opinion on history-taking, but cannot yet replace the full diagnostic
  encounter.'
