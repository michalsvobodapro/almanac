summaryDeep: 'This survey of endodontic practice patterns across France stratified
  practitioners into three profiles — endodontic specialists (ES), endodontically
  oriented practitioners (EO), and general practitioners (GP) — and compared their
  clinical activity, technology adoption, and care delivery. The differences are not
  marginal: specialists are far more likely to use rubber dam consistently, work under
  magnification, and employ advanced rotary systems, while GPs lag significantly on
  all three. The data raise a familiar but uncomfortable question about the standard
  of endodontic care patients receive depending on who treats them. For a dental student,
  this is a useful benchmark: the habits you build in school — rubber dam, magnification,
  proper irrigation — are the ones that separate competent from excellent endodontic
  outcomes throughout a career. The French context is not identical to Czech practice,
  but the specialty-versus-GP gap is likely universal.'
