summaryDeep: 'This is an opinion piece rather than primary data, but it names a real
  and underappreciated problem. The argument: endodontics has seen rapid commercialisation
  of reciprocating systems, bioceramic sealers, CBCT protocols, and regenerative techniques,
  often on the basis of in-vitro or short-term clinical data that would not satisfy
  regulatory thresholds in medicine. The ''innovation-validation gap'' is not unique
  to endodontics, but the specialty''s high procedure volume and the irreversibility
  of many interventions (extraction of a tooth that could have been saved, or retention
  of one that cannot) make the stakes particularly high. The editorial stops short
  of proposing specific regulatory mechanisms but calls on ESE and national societies
  to develop pre-market evidence standards analogous to medical device frameworks.
