summaryDeep: 'Pre- and postoperative CBCT scans from 32 single-tooth implant cases
  were registered to allow direct comparison of three planning approaches: clinically
  placed implants (HI-place), expert virtual plans (HI-plan), and AI-generated virtual
  plans (AI). Angular deviations relative to adjacent teeth and prosthetic wax-ups
  were statistically equivalent across all three methods (Friedman test, p>0.05),
  as were implant diameter and length selections. The AI system also demonstrated
  superior repeatability — a clinically relevant advantage when planning needs to
  be audited or revised. Proximity to vital structures and bone thickness measurements
  were similarly non-inferior. The study is limited by its small sample size (n=32)
  and restriction to single-tooth, straightforward cases; complex multi-unit or atrophic
  ridge scenarios were excluded. Still, for the bread-and-butter single implant, this
  is a meaningful validation that AI planning tools are no longer experimental. The
  time savings alone — planning time was significantly shorter with AI — have workflow
  implications for busy practices and teaching clinics.'
