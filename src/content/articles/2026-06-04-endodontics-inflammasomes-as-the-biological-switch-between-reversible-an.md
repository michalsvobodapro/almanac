  provocation — rather than biology. This review repositions inflammasomes as the

summaryDeep: 'The reversible/irreversible pulpitis binary has driven clinical decision-making
  for decades, yet it rests almost entirely on subjective symptom interpretation.
  This paper proposes that inflammasome complexes — particularly NLRP3 — act as central
  integrative hubs that sense cumulative pulpal stress and trigger the irreversible
  inflammatory cascade at a molecular level. The review synthesises evidence showing
  that inflammasome activation correlates with specific cytokine signatures and cell-death
  pathways that precede and predict clinical irreversibility. If validated in prospective
  studies, this framework could underpin chair-side biomarker tests that objectively
  stratify pulp status before treatment planning. For students learning vital pulp
  therapy indications, this is the mechanistic underpinning of why the same symptom
  profile can yield different histological outcomes — and why a purely symptom-based
  classification will always have a ceiling. It also opens a therapeutic angle: inflammasome
  inhibition as an adjunct to preserve pulp vitality longer.'
