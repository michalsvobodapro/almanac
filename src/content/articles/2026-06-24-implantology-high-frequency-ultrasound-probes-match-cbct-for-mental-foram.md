  Ultrasonographic measurements of horizontal and vertical MF diameters were obtained
  bilaterally using linear (3-12 MHz) and hockey-stick (8-18 MHz) probes and compared

summaryDeep: Accurate localisation of the mental foramen and its neurovascular bundle
  is critical for safe implant placement in the mandibular premolar region, and CBCT
  is the current standard. This study asks whether ultrasound — cheaper, radiation-free,
  and increasingly available — can provide comparable morphometric data. In 44 adults
  with bilaterally visible mental foramina on CBCT, both linear (3–12 MHz) and hockey-stick
  (8–18 MHz) probes were compared against CBCT reference measurements. Interobserver
  agreement was moderate to good (ICC 0.727–0.846) across modalities. After Benjamini-Hochberg
  correction, only one measurement (right horizontal, linear probe) remained significantly
  different from CBCT. The hockey-stick probe's smaller footprint and higher frequency
  gave it an edge in anatomical agreement. The study is small and cross-sectional,
  but the principle — ultrasound as a pre-implant screening tool to locate the mental
  foramen without radiation — is clinically attractive and worth watching.
