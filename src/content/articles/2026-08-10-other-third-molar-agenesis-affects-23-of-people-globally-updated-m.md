summaryDeep: 'Third molars are the teeth most frequently absent by agenesis, yet prevalence
  estimates have varied enormously across populations and study designs. This systematic
  review and meta-analysis searched five databases, including PubMed, Scopus, and
  SciElo, for radiographically diagnosed M3 agenesis in individuals aged ≥11 years,
  ultimately pooling 125 data points from 87,282 individuals. The random-effects pooled
  prevalence of 23.07% (95% CI: 21.2–25.0%) is robust in direction but comes with
  I²=97.6%, confirming that population-level variation is real and large. Sex-stratified
  analysis found slightly lower odds of agenesis in males (OR=0.84, 95% CI: 0.71–0.99);
