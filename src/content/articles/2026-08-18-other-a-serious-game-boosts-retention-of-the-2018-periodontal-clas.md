  posing challenges for dental students and educators. Serious games have emerged

  classification system. Materials and methods A randomised educational trial was

summaryDeep: 'The 2018 EFP/AAP periodontal classification is notoriously difficult
  to teach — complex staging and grading logic that students struggle to operationalise.
  This RCT randomised 46 dental students at Malmö to standard digital lectures alone
  or lectures plus a serious game app, then assessed knowledge at baseline and two
  weeks using two question formats. The game group outperformed controls on multiple-true-false
  questions (which demand finer discrimination) but not on single-best-answer items.
  Satisfaction was high in both groups and did not differ. The trial is small and
  single-centre, and the two-week window doesn''t tell us about long-term retention.
  But the directional finding is credible: game-based learning appears to add value
  specifically for the kind of nuanced classification logic the 2018 system demands.
  For dental educators — and for students self-studying — this is a practical, low-cost
  adjunct worth adopting.'
